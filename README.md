## What It Is

Qubic Church is a small, independent research group — one of a few efforts
studying Qubic and Aigarth, alongside the project's funded science work
(including Neuraxon) and source-of-truth tooling like the ANN Explorer. It does
not speak for Qubic and does not claim to be the source of truth. Its role is to
take the data around Qubic's AI work and make it open, readable, and
independently checkable — publishing its uncertainty honestly, and grading every
claim by confidence rather than overselling it.
